# Demo video guide

How to record the Hanko demo. The full voiceover lines live in
[`demo-script.md`](demo-script.md); this guide covers the setup, the style, the
shot list with timings, and the shorter cut. Aim for two to three minutes.

## Before you hit record

- **Screen record** hankolabs.xyz on devnet with QuickTime or Loom at 1080p, mic on.
- **Wallet:** Phantom set to Devnet, kept in a side panel so the tokens are visible.
- **Warm up first.** Do one full mint and refract before recording, so the faucet has
  already funded your wallet and the second run is instant with no waiting on airdrops.
- **Hide the onboarding** for a clean capture: the site respects
  `NEXT_PUBLIC_HIDE_ONBOARDING=1`, so the welcome modal and cookie banner stay out of
  frame. The production site already has them dismissed once you have visited.
- **Pace slower than feels natural.** Pause on every result. Let the named tokens sit
  on screen a beat longer than you want to. The product is the proof; give it room.

## Style

- Calm, plain voice. No slides, no hype. You are showing a working thing on devnet.
- One visual signature: open and close on the vermilion seal pressing in with a slight
  rotate. Use `public/brand/seal.svg` or the wordmark for the title and end card.
- Keep the cursor deliberate. Move, stop, click, wait. No fast scrolling.
- Colour discipline on screen matches the brand: the seal is the only warm mark, the
  Shield / Core / Edge spectrum is the data.

## Shot list

| Time | On screen | Beat |
| ---- | --------- | ---- |
| 0:00 to 0:12 | Seal title, then the hero | The hook: a stock price bundles safety, exposure and upside into one number, and you are forced to buy all three. |
| 0:12 to 0:28 | The three personas | Hanko refracts a share into Shield, Core and Edge. Saver, holder, believer. The three always recombine into one whole share. |
| 0:28 to 0:40 | A stock detail page | Live Pyth price, the same feed the vault settles from on-chain, over a real chart. Priced data doing real work. |
| 0:40 to 1:05 | Refract live: pick Tesla, mint, refract | Here it is on devnet. Pick a stock, mint a demo share, refract it into three tokens. |
| 1:05 to 1:20 | Cut to Phantom | The three tokens land in the wallet by name: Hanko TSLA Shield, Core, Edge. Real, tradeable. |
| 1:20 to 1:40 | Recombine, one line on trading a part | Sell one part, hold another, or recombine all three into a whole share. Fully backed, no leverage, no liquidation. |
| 1:40 to 2:05 | Pre-IPO detail, OpenAI via PreStocks | Not just public stocks. Pre-IPO too, with live PreStocks valuations, so a believer can hold Edge on OpenAI. |
| 2:05 to 2:20 | Press the seal, end on the hero | Hanko. Own only the part of a stock you want. Pyth-settled structured products on Solana, live on devnet. |

## The money moment

The single most important frame is the three named tokens appearing in Phantom
(1:05 to 1:20). Hold on it. That is what proves it is real and not a mockup.

## The 90-second cut

If you need it shorter, keep: hook, refract, named tokens in Phantom, recombine, and
one Pyth line. Drop the standalone trade step and the Pre-IPO section. The named
tokens moment stays no matter what.
